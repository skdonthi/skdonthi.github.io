Log 01 · Who's aboard

FULLSTACK ROOTS.
AGENTS IN PROD.
I OWN WHAT I ship.

A decade in product engineering taught me that great software starts with the people using it, not the framework you pick. I bring the same discipline to AI: understand the operator, the domain and the workflow first. Choose the model and the stack second.

At Xcontrol I sit aboard vessels with the crews who will run the system before I write a line of code. Discovery with operators and ship IT becomes the spec, then AI features that fit the workflow rather than the demo reel.

The domain has changed three times, from e-commerce platforms to reinsurance analytics to ships at sea. The method has not: production means observable, resilient, explainable, and fast enough that no one reaches for the spreadsheet instead.

Log 02 · Standing orders

HOW I work.

P-01

Be obsessed with your customer

I run discovery sessions with operators before writing a line of code. Real problems, not assumed ones, drive every architecture decision.

P-02

Raise the bar

Flakiness is a blocker, not an edge case. I hold production to a standard where observable, resilient, and fast are non-negotiable minimums.

P-03

Better together

The best outcomes come from engineering, operations, and customers in the same conversation. I work to close that gap, not widen it.

P-04

Act as an owner

I take end-to-end responsibility: from requirements through deployment, onboarding ship IT teams, and staying available when things break at sea.

P-05

Find joy in what you do

Whether debugging an agent loop or explaining RAG to a crew member who's never heard of an LLM, I find the work genuinely worth doing.

P-06

Your principle

What value matters most to your team? Click to add it. I'm here to adapt, grow, and build around what makes your organisation excellent.
